OUR STORY

A Church for People Like You

A prayer-based, Bible-centered, community-focused church in Brockton, Massachusetts.

WHO WE ARE

Faith that moves beyond Sunday.

After spending years as members and servants of Restoration Community Church in Brockton, Pastors Andrew and Patrice Ambroise felt called to change the way church and worship are traditionally done. Through prayer and reflection, they formed Hosanna International Ministries, now IGotoHim Church.

Our mission is simple: serve our community, spread God’s love, and teach God’s Word. All are welcomed. We’re not just church, we’re family.
