WATER BAPTISM

Baptism

Baptism is an outward declaration of an inward change. Going down into the water shows that your old life has been buried with Christ, and coming up shows that you have been raised to live a new life in Him.

“Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.”Romans 6:4 (KJV)

  • Who: Anyone who has accepted Jesus as Lord and Savior
  • What to wear: White clothing
  • Before: Candidates attend a foundational class on the significance and importance of baptism
  • Family: Invite your loved ones to celebrate with you

THE GOOD NEWS

Get Saved

God loves you and has a plan for your life. Salvation is a free gift, and you can receive it today, right where you are.

A

Admit

Admit that you are a sinner in need of a Savior.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.”Romans 3:23

B

Believe

Believe that Jesus died for your sins and that God raised Him from the dead.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”John 3:16

C

Confess

Confess Jesus as the Lord of your life.

“That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.”Romans 10:9

PRAY THIS PRAYER

A Prayer of Salvation

Lord Jesus, I know that I am a sinner and I need You. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and that God raised You from the dead. I turn from my sins and invite You into my heart and my life. I confess You as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me new life. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Did you pray this prayer?
